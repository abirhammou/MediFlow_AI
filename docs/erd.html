Boxes labelled "embeds" (DOCTOR_PROFILE, AVAILABILITY, EXTRACTED_FIELD) are sub-documents stored inside their parent, not separate collections. Every other link is an ObjectId reference.

Modelling choices

  • Roles: ADMIN, SECRETARY, DOCTOR, ADMIN_STAFF, PATIENT. A doctor is a user with an embedded doctorProfile and weekly availabilities. A PATIENT account points to its record with patientId.
  • Passwords are BCrypt hashes, refresh tokens are SHA-256 hashes. A TTL index deletes expired tokens on its own.
  • Patients are soft-deleted (deletedAt) so appointments and reports keep their history.
  • Summaries live in their own collection so every AI draft is kept. A partial unique index allows only one VALIDATED summary per patient, and patients.currentSummaryId points to it.
  • Extracted fields are embedded in their document because they are always read and validated together.
  • Record numbers (PAT-2026-00001) come from an atomic counter in counters.
